04 / THE THINKING BEHIND IT

Resilience is
more than
survival.

YOUNOVAL uses a preliminary six-dimension framework: customer, financial, marketing, operational, adaptive, and digital & learning resilience.

The framework and scores are educational self-assessment tools, not a validated prediction of business survival or a substitute for professional advice.
